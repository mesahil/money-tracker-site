Privacy policy

Money Tracker for Android · Effective 3 November 2026

Money Tracker is made by Sahil Pahuja, an individual developer in India (“I”, “me”). This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your financial data stays on your phone, encrypted. Nothing about your money is ever sent to me or anyone else.

What stays on your phone

Everything you record or the app captures is stored only on your device, in a database encrypted with a key held by Android’s secure keystore:

  • Transactions, accounts (name, type and optionally the last 4 digits), categories and investments you add.
  • Payment notifications, only if you turn on auto-capture and only from the apps you choose (for example your bank, UPI or SMS app). The app reads the amount, direction, merchant and reference from them. One-time passwords and non-payment notifications are ignored and never stored. The text of a notification is kept only until you approve or discard it, and cleared after 30 days. The app never asks for SMS permissions and cannot read your messages.
  • Voice commands. Speech is turned into text by your phone’s on-device speech recognition. The app doesn’t record or keep audio and doesn’t send it anywhere.

Your data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer. You can delete all of it at any time from Profile → Erase all data.

What leaves your phone

1. Anonymous usage statistics, only if you agree

The app asks once whether you’d like to share anonymous usage. It is off unless you say yes, and you can change your mind any time in Profile → Share anonymous usage. If you agree, the app sends:

  • which screens you open and which features you use (for example “a transaction was added by voice” or “a backup was created”), and when;
  • when the app is opened and closed, to measure how long it is used;
  • your phone model, Android and app version, language, time zone, and approximate country (worked out from your IP address, which is not stored);
  • a random identifier created by the app, so visits can be counted. It isn’t linked to your name, phone number, email or any account, and turning sharing off deletes it.

It never sends amounts, balances, merchants, notes, account details, category names, notification text, voice, or anything else you’ve recorded. The app doesn’t record your screen.

These statistics are processed by PostHog Inc. on servers in the European Union, on my behalf, and kept for up to 12 months. They are used only to understand which parts of the app are used and where it can be improved.

2. Update check

When the app starts, it asks the same service (PostHog, EU) whether this version is still supported, so it can tell you if an update is required. This request contains only the app’s public project key and a fixed identifier shared by every copy of the app. It identifies no one and records no usage. Like any internet request, it reveals your IP address to the server.

3. Backups and exports you share

If you create a backup or export your transactions, the file goes only where you send it, for example Google Drive or email. Backups can be protected with a passphrase. If you choose not to protect one, or export to CSV (spreadsheet), anyone with the file can read it, so keep it safe.

What I don’t do

  • No ads, and no advertising identifier.
  • No selling or renting of data, and no sharing with anyone except PostHog as described above.
  • No accounts, sign-in or cloud storage of your finances.
  • No investment or financial advice. Figures are only what you enter or the app works out from it.

Permissions

  • Notification access (optional): to read payment alerts from the apps you choose.
  • Microphone (optional): for voice commands, only while you’re using the mic button.
  • Fingerprint or screen lock (optional): for app lock.
  • Internet: only for the anonymous statistics (if you agree) and the update check.

Your rights

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can access, correct and erase your data and withdraw consent. Because your financial data is only on your phone, you control it directly in the app. To withdraw consent for usage statistics, turn off Profile → Share anonymous usage. For any question, request or complaint, write to the grievance contact below; I’ll reply within 30 days.

Children

Money Tracker is meant for people aged 18 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Anything that would send more data from your phone will be asked for in the app first.
